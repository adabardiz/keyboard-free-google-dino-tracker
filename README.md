Keyboard free Google Dino Tracker!

Play the classic Google Chrome Dinosaur game without touching your keyboard! This project uses computer vision to track your hand gestures & eye blinks to trigger jumps in the game. 
Built with Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, and PyAutoGUI.

Features:
Calculates the distance between your thumb and index finger. When you "pinch" or separate your fingers beyond a certain point, the dino jumps.
Uses facial landmark tracking to calculate the distance between your upper and lower eyelids. A quick blink will also trigger a jump.
Displays a live camera feed with your facial mesh, hand skeleton, and real time jump/walk statuses right on the screen.
